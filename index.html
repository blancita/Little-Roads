Little Roads

Drag across tiles to connect each house's driveway to the entrance of the building of its color. Tap a house to turn its driveway. Busy junctions slow cars, rush hour comes twice a day, and the map widens as the town spreads.

Every car carries one person (buses carry four), and each person who gets to work scores a point. Serve a building quickly and its happy customers add a bonus point. If a building piles up too many orders, its ring fills and you lose. Pinch to zoom. New maps unlock as you reach milestones.
